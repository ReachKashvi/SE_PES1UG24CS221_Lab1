# SE Lab 1 — Requirements Engineering & UML Use-Case Modelling

**Course:** Software Engineering — Dept. of CSE, PES University
**SRN:** PES1UG24CS221
**Problem Statement #34 — Retail, E-Commerce & Finance**
**Title:** Digital Gift Card & Loyalty Point Exchange

---

## Problem Context

A digital wallet platform that lets users aggregate brand loyalty points, convert
points into universal exchange credits at dynamic rates, and redeem digital gift
cards with anti-fraud voucher locking.

**Primary Actors:** Account Holder, Merchant Partner
